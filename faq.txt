CUSTOMER SERVICE FAQ

Q1. How can I track my order?
Answer: Sign in to your account and open the My Orders section. Select your order to view its status and tracking information.

Q2. What should I do if I was charged twice?
Answer: Check your bank statement and order history. Contact the payments support team with your order ID and evidence of both charges. Never share your PIN, password, or one-time password.

Q3. How can I request a refund?
Answer: Open your order and review the applicable refund policy. Submit a refund request with your order ID and a short explanation.

Q4. How do I change my account email address?
Answer: Sign in, open Account Settings, and follow the email verification steps.

Q5. I cannot sign in. What can I do?
Answer: Check your login details and use the password-reset option if necessary. Contact account support if the issue continues.

Q6. How can I contact customer support?
Answer: Open the Help or Contact Support section on the official website or application. Include a brief description of your issue.

Q7. What information is required for a missing order?
Answer: Provide your order ID, order date, and a description of the missing item.

Q8. Is my personal information safe?
Answer: Share only the information required to resolve your issue. Never share your password, PIN, or one-time password.
